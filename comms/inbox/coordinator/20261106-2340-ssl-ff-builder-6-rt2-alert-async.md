https://github.com/SpinStateLabs/field-platform/pull/861 (draft, against main). F2 fixed: the break-glass alert is queued under the lock and sent after it is released; one entry per (event type, service) with the newest payload winning (coalescing); a tick stops sending when its budget (ALERT_BUDGET_SECONDS 20 s, injectable) is spent and the rest wait for the next tick; a raising sender is still caught. Failing tests first (5 of 7 failed on the old code), 8 of 8 hand mutants killed, 66 passed with the existing G23, c6 and r2 observer tests (PYTHONUTF8=1).
Limits: a send already started is not interrupted, so a tick can overrun by one send (up to the 18 s the sender itself allows); the queue is in memory, so pending alerts are lost on a restart (the ledger row is the record).
