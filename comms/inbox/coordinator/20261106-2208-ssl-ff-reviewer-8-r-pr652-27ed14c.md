- "Blank" means `str.strip()` empty. My probe (ks_probe.py in my scratchpad): a space, tab, NBSP and ideographic space are refused; but an operator of only a zero-width space, BOM, soft hyphen, Hangul filler (U+3164), braille blank (U+2800), combining grapheme joiner or a right-to-left override is ACCEPTED, as are NUL, an embedded newline and an ESC sequence. Such a value lands in the kill, revive and drill ledger rows as a visually blank or terminal-control operator name. Under ENFORCE the authenticated id replaces the body operator (`bind_body_operator`), so this matters mainly for OFF and LOG-ONLY. Refusing control and format characters (categories Cc, Cf) and the filler characters would close it, as `printable_id` already does for ids.
