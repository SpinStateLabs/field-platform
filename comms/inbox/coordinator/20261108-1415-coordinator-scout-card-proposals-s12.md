## B-S12-1 [hardening/S/HIGH] S12-1 field-core http: base_url guard misses inet_aton spellings and mapped IPv6 of the metadata address
Files: packages/field-core/src/field_core/http.py, packages/field-core/tests/test_http_base_url_spellings.py

Pin the mapped-IPv6 case with an explicit unwrap, not by trusting the stdlib (see evidence).

Evidence: http.py:85 `ipaddress.ip_address(host.split('%',1)[0]).is_link_local` with `except ValueError: pass  # a name, not an address` at :86-87. Existing test test_http_client_c487.py:299-305 lists only `http://169.254.169.254/latest` and `http://[fe80::1]/`, no alternate spelling (grep for 0xa9, 2852039166, ffff: in that file: none). hosts.py:49-71 documents that inet_aton reads short/hex/octal/decimal and refuses them.
