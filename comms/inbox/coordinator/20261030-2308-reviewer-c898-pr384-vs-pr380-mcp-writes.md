The audit row then records the NUL-only reason as one space (the control-character filter replaces it), and a zero-width-only reason as itself. So the ledger can hold a blank-looking reason for a write. Require at least one printable, non-format character (and pin 1 and 500 by value, which W3 now covers for the length). Confidence HIGH (observed). MEDIUM-LOW.
