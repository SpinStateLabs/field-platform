FINDINGS
F-C1074-409-1 MEDIUM (test gap, class: the invariant is not pinned), _transport.py:54-62 vs test_read_cap_c642b.py:16-23. 19 mutants: 13 RED, 6 GREEN against the PR's tests AND against the whole field-agent suite (re-run from the package directory after a baseline check): (i) per-chunk count instead of cumulative: on a real socket a 3 MiB chunked body is then ACCEPTED in full; (ii) body keeps only the first chunk: a 300 KiB chunked reply comes back as 65,442 bytes, silently; (iii) the streaming path never used (always the after-the-fact check): a 64 MiB chunked reply is read to the end (server wrote 64.0 MiB) before the refusal, against 1.3 MiB unmutated; (iv) framing headers kept (_FRAMING_HEADERS, line 19): a gzip JSON reply INSIDE the cap fails with a DecodingError; (v) duplicate headers collapsed (multi_items): LOW; (vi) the cap value 1 MiB unpinned (tests use the symbol): LOW. Cause: MockTransport yields one chunk (see objection 1). Fix: one test whose transport yields an endless or multi-chunk generator (assert ResponseTooLarge and the bytes pulled stay near the cap, which kills i, iii), one multi-chunk body under the cap returned whole (kills ii), one gzip reply under the cap returned decoded (kills iv), and the number 1048576 written once in a test.
F-C1074-409-2 LOW-MEDIUM (resource, class: bound enforced after the decode step), _transport.py:54-56. A gzip body of 65 KB (64 MiB decoded) and one of 521 KB (512 MiB decoded) are both refused correctly; peak Python allocation during the call is 141.5 MiB and 141.8 MiB: the bound is about the raw read chunk (64 KiB) times deflate's ratio, per call, and concurrent calls add up. The commit text "a compressed bomb cannot bypass MAX_RESPONSE_BYTES" holds for the returned size, not for the transient memory. Decision for Don's lane: accept and document, or bound the decode step.
