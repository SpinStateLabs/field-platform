## Objections first (FORCE)
1. The shared deadline is taken AFTER the interrupts are sent but BEFORE any session is polled, so a slow `events.send` for many sessions eats into the wait; the total /halt time is then the interrupt phase plus about `wait`, not `wait` alone. Intended per the one-line change, but the test does not bound the interrupt phase (the fake sends are instant).
2. Sessions late in the list get less (possibly zero) polling time: once the deadline passes, each later session gets exactly one `retrieve` and is archived only if it is already archivable. A session that would have gone idle within its own full `wait` is now left `running`. This is the intended trade (N x wait was the bug) but no test covers "later session becomes idle inside the shared window and is archived" (mutant J7 below).
3. The PR body leaves open whether the kill-switch caller's timeout is shorter than 20 s (not checked by the PR; not in this PR's scope; not in source).
