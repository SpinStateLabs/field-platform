## Console: attestation pack and source-query links (security guidance for B-CONSOLE-attestation-links)
Current renderer (`attestation_reporter/render.py` `render_html`) escapes every field (`html.escape`, quotes on). Making `source_query` clickable adds an injection surface; keep these:
1. Build the `<a href>` from a parsed URL, not the raw string: allow only `http`/`https`, and only the known service host:port or console route; refuse `javascript:`, `data:`, other schemes, userinfo (`user:pass@`), and a host that is not the configured service base. Escape the href and the text with `html.escape(..., quote=True)`; add `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. A test with `javascript:alert(1)`, `"><script>`, `http://evil@127.0.0.1/` and a `;`-joined list holding one bad element.
2. The pack JSON is signed: leave `source_query` untouched in the JSON and split the `;` list only in the HTML rendering, or the signature or `attest verify` breaks.
3. `GET http://127.0.0.1:8002/events?...` is the INTERNAL service address and needs the perimeter secret header, so a bare link answers 401 to a reader and prints the internal topology in a board document. Link through the console (an authenticated route that shows the query result), or print a relative console path; do not link service ports.
4. The served `/attest/pack.html` is behind the perimeter secret and is served by Caddy prefix, not by ops-console (no route for it in ops_console/api.py); the "attestation pack does not work" symptom is outside the console code. A reproduction needs the estate (Caddy prefix, reporter signing key, FIELD_SHARED_SECRET through the console download button, `a.download` at console.html:1276). Not reproduced locally (no estate); LOW confidence on the root cause.
