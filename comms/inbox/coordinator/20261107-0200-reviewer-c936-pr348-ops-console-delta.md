The single-id URL routes are safe because the console's own router decodes `%2F` first and 404s (pinned by test_an_encoded_slash_in_an_id_never_reaches_the_handler_or_the_upstream). The PR's test `test_a_slash_or_odd_character_in_a_bulk_id_or_a_halt_domain_stays_inside_one_segment` (test_proxy_hardening_c554.py:298-305) pins the raw wire bytes (`a%2Fb`), which is the wrong property: it passes while the route changes.
