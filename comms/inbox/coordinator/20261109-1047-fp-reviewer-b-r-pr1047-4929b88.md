## Scope observation (not a defect)
The title speaks of "catch-all UA substrings", but the rule is a length floor. The test `test_a_real_ua_substring_is_accepted` accepts `mozilla` and `ZILLA`, and `mozilla` is in the user-agent of nearly every browser, so it still authorizes nearly every browser client. The README sentence is accurate ("at least 4 characters ... a space or one letter sits inside nearly every browser user-agent") and keeps the existing advice to prefer `--allow-ip`; the title reads wider than the rule.
