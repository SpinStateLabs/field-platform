## Code read
- AppendRequest caps: caller_id 200, caller_ts 64, caller_signature 200; over the cap is the fixed no-echo 422 (the test asserts the body stays under 1,000 characters and exactly-at-limit values are not size-refused).
- `_shown_caller_id` shows `repr` of the first 64 characters plus the length when cut; short ids are shown whole and unchanged.
- The caller id grammar is `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9-]{0,62}$` (store.py:143), so a value reaches the "key file" refusal sites (missing file, unreadable, not Ed25519, replay window, bad signature) only when it is at most 63 characters. Those five sites are therefore already bounded; wrapping them is defence in depth, not a measured fix. The two sites that matter (invalid id, no keyring) are the ones with tests.
- The remaining `{caller_id}.pub.pem` in the bad-signature text (store.py:475) is reached only for a valid id, so it is bounded too.
