## Findings
- F-C593-1 LOW to MEDIUM (a gap in the NEW srcset rule): the entry-start rule requires a comma followed by whitespace (`,[[:space:]]+`), but a valid `srcset` needs no space after a comma: `srcset="a.png 1x,https://evil.example/b.png 2x"` and `srcset="a.png 1x,//evil.example/b.png"` (rc 0 in my probe) pass the guard; so does a `srcset` whose value begins with a space after the quote (`srcset=" https://evil.example/a.png 1x"`). The old pattern caught these (it accepted any `//` in the value) at the price of the false positives. Fix: `,[[:space:]]*` is NOT enough on its own (a data URI such as `data:image/png;base64,AAAA//8=` then matches after its own comma), so require the entry start to be preceded by a comma and followed directly by a scheme or `//` only when the text before the comma ends a descriptor (`[0-9.]+[wx]`) or the start of the value: `([^"'>]*[0-9]+[wx][[:space:]]*,[[:space:]]*)?` and allow leading spaces after the opening quote (`["']?[[:space:]]*`).
- F-C593-2 LOW (3 surviving mutants): add samples so removing the word-start anchor from `data=` and from `srcset`, or letting `data` take spaces again, goes red: `xdata=//x` and `xsrcset=//x` (must not match), `let data = // c` and `data = https://example.org` (must not match; the PR lists the shipping and mapping forms only).
