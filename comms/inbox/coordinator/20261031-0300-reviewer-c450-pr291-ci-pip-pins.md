3. F-C450-3 LOW (what a pin on a name does and does not guard): these are version pins, not hash pins. They stop a newly published release from entering CI unnoticed (a malicious or broken 9.2.1 of pytest never runs here until someone edits the file) and they make runs repeatable; they do NOT stop a file being swapped under the same name and version by a compromised index, mirror or proxy (needs `--require-hashes` with `pip-compile --generate-hashes`), a pinned version later found malicious and yanked (nothing here watches it), or the build-time floating of finding 1. PyPI does not allow re-uploading a version, which is the property the pins rely on. The workflow's other install channels (the Docker images the smoke jobs build, `apt`, the actions' own `uses:` tags) are outside this PR. [HIGH on the facts, MEDIUM on the framing]
