## Bottleneck: the dispatcher cannot skip a card the caller must not take (measured)
`dispatch.sh next` claims the first unclaimed row for the role. A reviewer who already gave the first verdict on a two-verdict security card (R-PR1087-bd387d7) gets that same card back on every `next` after releasing it. Workaround I used: release only after filling width. Proposal: `next` skips rows whose target already has a done/lock file whose Owner equals <who>, or the WORKLIST row carries an `exclude=<who>` note.

## Bottleneck: duplicate rows per head
WORKLIST has rows for superseded heads (R-PR1084-3eb989b, R-PR1085-3e74a31, R-PR1087-4c5c75f, R-PR1082-11fcaba, R-PR1083-8d2a59f) after their PRs were recommitted or merged. Each costs a claim and a report. Proposal: when a builder enqueues a new head, mark the old head's row `superseded-by <sha7>` so `next` skips it, or the restock step drops rows whose PR is merged or whose head moved.
