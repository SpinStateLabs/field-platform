# C-230 reviewer report (Lane 5): services/spend-governor mutation round on main 10fb197

VERDICT: the cap, window and throttle arithmetic is pinned: 59 of 75 mutants killed, and every one of the 16 survivors is either equivalent (13) or in an untested side path (3, none of them a way to spend past a cap). The governor is a meter, not a gate, and behaves exactly so: it records spend after a cap is reached and says BLOCK; refusing is the sentinel's job. One probe finding on the unmutated code: an integer at or above 2 to the power 63 makes `POST /spend` answer 500, and two large but valid amounts make an agent's `/status` answer 500 for the whole window (MEDIUM-LOW, proven). Three test gaps and one visibility gap are listed.

FINDING F-C230-1 (MEDIUM-LOW, PROVEN): integer range. `POST /spend` with `cents` of 9223372036854775807 (2 to the 63 minus 1) is accepted (201); with 2 to the 63 or 10 to the 19 it answers 500 Internal Server Error (an unhandled SQLite OverflowError, not a 422); and after one accepted maximal row, a second maximal amount overflows the `SUM` and `GET /status/<agent>` answers 500 for as long as the rows are in the window (daily window: a day). The consumer (the sentinel's spend client) treats a governor error as BLOCK (C-168), so the effect is a self-inflicted lockout of that agent plus a poisoned window, not a bypass; it needs the perimeter secret. Fix: bound `cents`, `tokens`, `actions` (for example `le=10**12`) in the request model so the answer is 422. [HIGH, run]
